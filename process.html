From inquiry to service

A calmer path from “we’re planning” to “dinner is served.”

This demo shows how a catering business can explain its process without promising a date, confirming an order or taking payment.

Illustrative workflow

Know what comes next.

Client-specific proposal, ordering, signature and deposit tools are scoped separately and require provider credentials plus end-to-end testing.

Inquiry

Visitors share event date, estimated guest count, location, format, budget, menu interests and dietary needs.

Discovery

The caterer confirms whether the event may be a fit and asks any needed venue or service questions.

Secure proposal

An approved client system can deliver scope, pricing, policies and optional selections privately.

Booking

Only the real provider workflow should handle acceptance, signatures, deposits and confirmation.

Allergens & accommodations

A placeholder for the client’s real safety practices.

A live catering site must describe only the procedures the client actually follows. This template does not state that any kitchen, dish or event is allergen-free.

Can dietary preferences be accommodated?

Sample answer: Share preferences in the inquiry. The caterer should confirm available options after reviewing the menu, guest count and service format.

What about serious food allergies?

Sample answer: Identify allergens and severity before ordering. The client must replace this with its confirmed cross-contact, sourcing and kitchen policy. No accommodation is guaranteed by this demo.

Can menu items be labeled?

Sample answer: Labeling may be proposed for the event, but ingredient and cross-contact information must be verified by the real caterer.

When is the date confirmed?

Only after the caterer reviews the inquiry and the client’s approved booking process is completed. Submitting the demo form does not hold or confirm a date.
