Service formats

Four ways the food can arrive.

Service level affects staffing, timing, venue requirements and price. The inquiry asks visitors to choose a starting preference without confirming it.

Lowest serviceDelivery / drop-off

Prepared food delivered in presentation-ready or transport-safe packaging. Setup and pickup terms are client-defined.

FlexibleStyled buffet

A composed buffet with setup, serviceware and optional attendants based on the confirmed proposal.

InteractiveFood stations

Multiple menu moments that encourage movement and may include chef-attended finishing.

Full servicePlated meal

Courses served to each guest with a staffing and rental plan tailored to the venue and timeline.
