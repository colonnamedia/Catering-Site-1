Dietary & allergen note

Accommodations begin with a conversation.

Use this area for the client’s confirmed allergen processes, kitchen limitations and accommodation policies. The demo does not promise an allergen-free environment. Visitors should identify allergies and dietary needs in the inquiry, and the caterer must confirm what can be safely accommodated before ordering.

Sample labels such as vegetarian, vegan, gluten-aware or dairy-aware must be verified against the client’s actual recipes, sourcing and cross-contact practices before launch.
